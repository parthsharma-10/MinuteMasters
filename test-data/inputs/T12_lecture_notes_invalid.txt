Lecture 12: Second Law of Thermodynamics and Carnot Cycle.
The efficiency of a reversible heat engine operating between two thermal reservoirs at temperatures Th and Tc depends solely on the temperatures.
Formula: eta = 1 - (Tc / Th).
Key concept: Entropy is a state function representing microscopic disorder.
